What we do for you

One local partner for the whole show

We handle the artist, the sound system, the permits, the promotion and the box office. You get a signed contract and a fixed price in CUP, MLC or EUR.

Live music for hotels

Weekly or one-off performances for lobbies, pool areas, à la carte restaurants and special evenings. Trios, quartets or full dance bands.

Full event production

Stage, line-array sound, lighting, backline, power generator and technical crew. Up to 8 000 people outdoors.

Cultural experiences

Private son and rumba sessions, percussion workshops, décima improvisation and guided musical walks in Trinidad's historic centre.

Sound & lighting rental

Professional audio, wireless microphones, moving lights and staging, with operators included.

Recording & licensing

Our own studio in Sancti Spíritus for albums, jingles and location recording, plus digital distribution.

Weddings & private parties

All-inclusive packages: band, DJ, master of ceremonies, decoration, photography and catering coordination.
